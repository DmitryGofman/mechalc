What the colors mean: every zone is painted by its own check — neutral green far from yield, amber at half margin, red at the limit. The crescent at each hole is bearing; the ligament to the short edge is tear-out; the flanks beside the hole are the net section; the pin lights up at the shear planes and, in a clevis, in bending between them. The visible slip and shear-step are exaggerated (~40× on a typical steel joint) so you can see where the joint is heading. Static yield-onset checks — typical values, verify before production.
