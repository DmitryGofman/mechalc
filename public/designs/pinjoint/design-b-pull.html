Reading the ladder: everything below your dashed line has already yielded; the first rung above it is your margin. The right joint has its whole ladder comfortably above the working load — if the first rung is a plate mode, the flanges are the weak link (thicken, widen, or move the hole off the edge); if it's a pin mode, size up or harden the pin. Static yield-onset checks, no stress concentration or preload friction — typical values, verify before production.
