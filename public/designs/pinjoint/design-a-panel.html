Model notes: static checks at yield onset, straight from Shigley Fig 8-23 — pin shear on 1 or 2 planes vs 0.577·Sy, clevis pin bending M = F/2·(t₂/4 + gap + t₁/2), bearing over the projected area t·d both on the plate hole and on the pin, net-section tension across (w−d)·t, and edge tear-out on the two ligaments 2·t·(a−d/2). No stress concentration (Kt≈2–3 at the hole — add it for fatigue or brittle plates), no preload friction. Typical reference values — verify before production use.
