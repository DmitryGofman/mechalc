How the pieces interconnect: torque sets bolt preload; preload both grips the cylinder and bends the ears and ovalizes the tube; those deflections eat the flange gap; when the gap closes the grip stops growing no matter how hard you tighten. Plastics then quietly give back 40–55% of the preload to creep — the hero number is the long-term grip. Typical reference values; verify before production use.
