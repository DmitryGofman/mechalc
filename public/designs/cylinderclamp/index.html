MECHCALC · DESIGN STUDY

Cylinder Clamp — Split Pillow Block

Four interaction prototypes for one calculator: a printed or machined two-piece clamp gripping a cylinder (solid or hollow) with 2–6 bolts. Same physics engine under all four (clamp-engine.js) — what differs is how you drive it.
DESIGN A

Control Panel

The toolkit's classic form: every input on the left, every consequence stacked on the right, deformed cross-section below. Nothing hidden, nothing sequenced.

for: final design checks, documenting a chosen setup
DESIGN B

Tightening Simulator

One big torque slider is the wrench. An event ladder lists — in torque order — grip goal met, ears yield, tube crushes, gap bottoms, bolt proof. Find the torque window between "holds" and "breaks".

for: picking a torque spec, building intuition
DESIGN C

Bolt-Count Advisor

Inverted flow: state the job (axial load, torque, safety factor) and it works backwards to required torque for 2 vs 4 vs 6 bolts, side by side, with each configuration's failure checks. Answers "do I need two or four?"

for: the first minutes of a new design
DESIGN F

3D Clamp · grab it

The assembly in 3D — orbit it, drag a bolt head to tighten, watch the ears bend and the flange gap close until it shuts. Every surface painted by its own safety factor. Half-section and force arrows on toggles.

for: seeing what the numbers mean, showing someone else
DESIGN E

Statics View · phone-first

The whole clamp reduced to the statics-course free-body diagram: cap as a beam, cylinder as the pin support (triangle), bolts as two closing forces — with the live bending-moment diagram. The most compact of the five.

for: quick checks on a phone, thinking in first principles
DESIGN D

Live Section

The deformed cross-section is the interface: click the tube, ears or bolts to edit that part; drag torque and watch ears bend, the tube ovalize and the gap close, every part painted with its own safety factor.

for: exploring geometry, explaining a failure to someone
THE SHARED MODEL — HOW EVERYTHING INTERCONNECTS
wrench torque ─► preload F=T/(K·d) ─► clamp force ─► pressure p=ΣF/(D·W) ─► grip η·μ·π·ΣF │ │ ├─► ear bending σ=6Fe/(b·tf²) ──┐ ├─► tube hoop pR/t + ovalization ├─► head bearing on plastic │ │ └─► bolt von Mises vs proof ▼ ▼ gap budget: g − (2δ_ear + δ_oval) gap closes ⇒ grip stops growing plastics: creep gives back 40–55% of preload ⇒ every grip number shown fresh AND long-term

The five failure modes checked everywhere: flange (ear) bending in the clamp; bearing crush under the bolt head (the printed-part killer — washers double-checked); tube wall crush/ovalization for hollow cylinders; bolt proof stress; and slip — grip below the duty. Plus the geometric one: the flange gap bottoming out, after which torque stops buying grip entirely.

Materials: PC-ABS, PLA, PETG, ASA, Nylon 12 (FDM & MJF) with in-plane strengths and creep-retention factors; aluminum 5052 / 6061-T6; mild and alloy steel. Cylinders: steel/stainless/aluminum tube or solid rod, hard chromed rod. All typical reference values — verify before production use.
