TORQUE/BOLT
Cylinder · bolts · duty
The model in one line: each bolt force F sits at a = R+e from the middle; the bore pushes back spread over ±R; so the crown carries M = F·(e + R/2), and the crown section b·tc²/6 must take it. The ears carry their own cantilever moment F·e on tf. Everything else — grip, gap, tube crush, creep — is inherited from the same shared engine. Typical reference values; verify before production.
