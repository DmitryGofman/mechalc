DESIGN STUDY · BRAND

MechCalc — four logo candidates

Each mark is built from the toolkit's own visual language — the blueprint blue, the green-to-red yield palette, engineering-drawing conventions — and shown where it will actually live: the iPhone home screen, the browser tab, the app header. Stress Beam is currently active; switching is one constant in src/brand.tsx plus one script run.

A

Blueprint

A bolt cross-section drawn to drafting convention — body circle, ¾ thread arc, dash-dot centerlines on a drawing grid. The quietest and most "engineering document" of the four; reads instantly as a mechanical drawing.

Hex Monogram

A bold M inside a hex-nut outline — the classic app-icon play: simplest silhouette, strongest at a distance, most conventional. The M is drawn with strokes, not a font, so it renders identically everywhere down to 16 px.
