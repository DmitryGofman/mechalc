DESIGN STUDY · BRAND

MechCalc — logo candidates

Each mark is built from the toolkit's own visual language — the blueprint blue, the green-to-red yield palette, engineering-drawing conventions — and shown where it will actually live: the iPhone home screen, the browser tab, the app header. Moment Field (B4) is the shipped logo; the rest stay here as the study that chose it. Switching is one constant in src/brand.tsx plus one script run.

ROUND 2 · STRESS BEAM VARIANTS

Tension / compression studies

Four takes on the chosen beam, painted by signed bending stress using the app's own scale from stressColor.ts: tension warms green → amber → red above the neutral axis, compression cools green → teal → blue below it, and the neutral axis runs through the middle as a drawing-convention dash-dot centerline.

B1

Split Fibers

The boldest reading: solid tension-red top flange, compression-blue bottom flange, dark core between — and the neutral axis dash-dotted in green, the scale's zero-stress color. The flattest and most poster-like of the four.
