MECHALC · DESIGN STUDIES

Cantilever beam, six ways

The same calculator — end-loaded rectangular cantilever, k = 3EI/L³, σ = 3Etδ/2L², safety factor against yield — rebuilt six times, each in the design language of an Apple Design Award winner. Same math, six moods.

✓
AChecklist
after Things · ADA 2017
Calm white space, grouped rows, and results as to-dos that tick themselves off. A Things-style progress pie fills as the margin reaches ×2.
✈
BFlight Deck
after Flighty · ADA 2023
Live-tracking dashboard: FIX→TIP route with a stress-gradient beam, status banner, tabular numerals, and a flight-phase timeline of checks.
◉
CPlayful
after (Not Boring) Habits · ADA 2024
The safety factor fills the screen in huge rounded numerals. Grab the tip and bend the beam — it springs back with a wobble; the sky changes with the margin.
◐
DInstrument
after Halide · ADA 2020
A pro camera for stress: viewfinder with grid and focus peaking on over-yield fibers, a live σ(x) histogram, and scrub-wheel dials for every input.
#
ENotebook
after Bear · ADA 2017
Typography-first worksheet on warm paper: the inputs are blanks in the prose, the working shows every step, and the verdict reads like a margin note.
⛅
FStress Forecast
after CARROT Weather · ADA 2020
A weather app for your beam: current conditions = peak stress, the hourly strip = stations along the span, the 10-day = every material's outlook. Snark included.
All six run the same closed-form model as src/calculators/FlexureCalc.tsx (linear Euler–Bernoulli, materials from the shared library). They are standalone HTML files with no dependencies — open any of them straight from disk.
